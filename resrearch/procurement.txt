# 🏗️ NATIONAL HOUSING PROCUREMENT STRATEGY

## “Standardised Modular Housing Platform (Model B)”

***

# 1. 🎯 CORE PROCUREMENT PRINCIPLE

> Government defines the **product, price, and standards**, and controls access to demand — while the private sector delivers supply competitively.

***

## ✅ Procurement rule

Any contractor can participate if they can deliver:

```
• Approved housing specification
• Within total cost cap (~$350k)
• At required quality + performance standard
```

***

# 2. 🏡 STANDARD HOUSING PRODUCT (FIXED SPEC)

## ✅ National housing standard

All dwellings must meet:

```
• 3 bedrooms
• 2 living areas:
   - Primary living (main lounge)
   - Secondary living (kitchen-adjacent / flexible family)
• 2 bathrooms (MANDATORY)
• Kitchen + dining hub
• Laundry
• Detached, single-storey
• ~140–160 sqm internal area
• ~550 sqm lot (planning-flexible baseline)
```

***

## 🧠 Strategic intent

* Single national housing “SKU”
* Eliminates design fragmentation
* Enables industrial production

***

# 3. 🧩 MODULAR DESIGN PLATFORM

## 3.1 Standardised module system

All homes must be constructed using a defined set of modules:

```
• Bedroom module (x3)
• Main living module
• Secondary living module
• Kitchen module
• Bathroom module A (main)
• Bathroom module B (ensuite/second bathroom)
• Circulation module
• Laundry module
• Optional garage module
```

***

## ✅ Rules

* Modules must be dimensionally standardised
* Interchangeable across builders
* Compatible with multiple construction methods

***

## 🧠 System objective

> Create a **shared component ecosystem**, allowing multiple firms to collaborate and specialise.

***

# 4. 🏗️ CONSTRUCTION COMPATIBILITY (DUAL DELIVERY MODEL)

All contractors must deliver identical functional housing outcomes using one of three pathways:

***

## ✅ A. Prefabricated / modular construction

* Factory-built panels or volumetric modules
* On-site assembly
* High-repeatability manufacturing

***

## ✅ B. Onsite systemised construction

* Standardised components
* Conventional or semi-industrial methods

***

## ✅ C. Automated / 3D-assisted construction

* Printed or automated structural shell
* Modular interior fit-out

***

## ✅ Constraint

```
Final product must meet identical:
• layout
• size
• functionality
• quality thresholds
```

***

## 🧠 Result

> Competing production methods delivering the same product → drives efficiency and innovation.

***

# 5. 🛠️ CONTRACTOR PROCUREMENT MODEL

## 5.1 Open-access participation

Contractors may submit bids such as:

```
“We will deliver 500 compliant homes at $345k each”
```

***

## ✅ Eligibility requirements

* Compliance with standard housing spec
* Demonstrated capability (financial + delivery)
* Cost disclosure submission
* Agreement to performance and inspection regime

***

## ✅ Evaluation criteria

* Price (primary)
* Delivery speed
* Quality / defect history
* Scalability

***

## 🧠 Key mechanism

> Contractors compete on **execution efficiency**, not design variation.

***

# 6. 💰 COST CONTROL SYSTEM (PBS-STYLE)

## 6.1 Price cap

```
Total cost cap ≈ $350k per dwelling (land + build)
```

***

## 6.2 Mandatory cost disclosure

Contractors must provide:

```
• Labour costs
• Material cost bands
• Overhead + margin
```

***

## ✅ Government uses this to:

* Benchmark real market costs
* Identify inefficiencies
* Adjust price caps over time

***

## 🧠 Equivalent to:

> PBS “price disclosure” → continuous downward pressure on cost

***

# 7. 🌍 LAND PROCUREMENT STRATEGY

## 7.1 Core principle

> Government captures land value early and controls release to eliminate speculative pricing.

***

## ✅ A. Englobo acquisition

* Acquire large parcels pre-rezoning
* Use:
  * negotiated purchase
  * strategic acquisition
  * compulsory acquisition where required

***

## ✅ B. Planning + subdivision

Government controls:

```
• zoning
• infrastructure provision
• subdivision planning
```

***

## ✅ C. Land release

Land is:

```
• Only released into Income Title system
• Not sold into open speculative market
```

***

## ✅ Outcome

```
Land cost controlled at ~$100k per lot
```

***

# 8. 🏗️ INTEGRATED LAND + BUILD MODELS

## Option 1 — Government-led land, private build

* Government provides serviced lots
* Contractors build homes

***

## Option 2 — Private integrated delivery

Developer proposes:

```
• Land + infrastructure + housing
• Delivered within total cost cap
```

***

## ✅ Both allowed

→ increases competition, flexibility

***

# 9. 🏭 INDUSTRIAL SUPPLY CHAIN STRUCTURE

Because of standardisation:

***

## ✅ Enables:

* Modular factories (bathroom pods, wall panels, kitchens)
* Specialist component suppliers
* Repeatable assembly processes

***

## ✅ Ecosystem effect

Different firms can specialise:

```
• Bathroom module manufacturer
• Structural panel supplier
• Assembly contractor
```

***

👉 Creates **manufacturing-style supply chain**

***

# 10. 🔁 CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT LOOP

## Step 1 — Collect cost data

## Step 2 — Benchmark across builders

## Step 3 — Identify efficiency leaders

## Step 4 — Tighten:

```
• price caps
• design tolerances
• performance standards
```

***

## 🧠 Result

> Costs fall over time through competition + scale

***

# 11. ⚖️ RISK MANAGEMENT

## ❌ Risk: underbidding / contractor failure

✅ Fix:

* staged payments
* performance bonds

***

## ❌ Risk: quality erosion

✅ Fix:

* strict inspection regime
* defect liability periods

***

## ❌ Risk: cartel behaviour

✅ Fix:

* open-access procurement
* multiple suppliers

***

## ❌ Risk: design stagnation

✅ Fix:

* controlled minor layout variants (5–10)

***

# 12. 🔥 SYSTEM OUTCOME

This procurement model creates:

***

## 🏗️ Industrial housing production

* Repeatable product
* scalable supply

***

## 💰 Cost discipline

* Fixed price cap
* continuous cost benchmarking

***

## 🧠 Competitive innovation

* prefab vs onsite vs 3D competition
* no locked-in method

***

## 🌍 Land value control

* eliminates speculation
* aligns land cost with housing affordability

***

# ✅ FINAL SYNTHESIS

> The procurement system combines a single standardised, modular housing product (3-bed, 2 living, 2 bath) with an open-access, price-capped contractor model and government-controlled land supply, enabling multiple construction methods to compete within a tightly defined specification—thereby industrialising housing delivery while maintaining cost discipline and scalability.

***

# ✅ ONE-LINE CONCLUSION

> By fixing the housing product and price while leaving delivery open to competing contractors, the system uses PBS-style demand control to drive industrial-scale, modular housing production at predictable cost without requiring the government to directly build or supply inputs.
