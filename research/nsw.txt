NSW Housing Pattern Book, which is part of the broader Low and Mid-Rise Housing Policy introduced by the NSW Government. It is specifically intended to standardise and accelerate the delivery of medium-density and mid-rise housing.

What it does

The Pattern Book provides pre-designed, architect-endorsed building templates that can be reused across NSW rather than every project starting from scratch. The designs are intended to be adaptable to specific sites while retaining a standardised approval pathway.
