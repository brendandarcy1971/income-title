## Speaker Notes (Ready to Paste)
This slide answers a simple question: what exactly are we delivering?

Income Title is not a broad subsidy for whatever the market supplies. It is a defined home product with fixed minimum standards.

That matters for execution. Standardisation improves procurement comparability, reduces design churn, and helps keep quality and cost inside clear bounds.

For households, these are normal family homes: three bedrooms, two bathrooms, two living zones, single-storey form, and practical liveability.

For government and delivery partners, the product is measurable and auditable. That supports faster approvals, cleaner tendering, and reliable scale-up.
