# Slide 7.1 Plan: Targeted Eligibility - Who Gets Income Title

## Slide Objective
Show that Income Title is a targeted access pathway for households excluded from ownership, not a universal subsidy.

## Time Allocation
- Target talk time: 1.5 minutes.
- Audience outcome: They should understand who qualifies, why, and how fairness is protected.

## Core Message
Income Title is reserved for market-excluded households, especially long-term renters under sustained affordability stress.

## Supporting Proof Points
- Eligibility is based on demonstrated exclusion from ownership.
- Long-term renters are prioritised through transparent scoring.
- Anti-gaming rules prevent windfall capture.
- Allocation remains auditable and regionally balanced.

## Recommended Slide Headline
Targeted Access: Reserved for Households Locked Out of Ownership

## Recommended Subheadline
This is not universal demand subsidy. It is a rules-based pathway for long-term renters and market-excluded households.

## Visual Concept
- Primary visual: Eligibility funnel (Applicants -> Eligible -> Prioritised -> Allocated).
- Secondary visual: Scoring card (years renting, rent burden, household needs, local connection).
- Optional visual: Exclusion guardrail box.

## Layout (16:9)
- Top 18%: headline + subheadline.
- Left 50%: eligibility funnel.
- Right 50%: criteria and anti-gaming safeguards.
- Bottom strip: fairness takeaway.

## On-Slide Copy (Draft)
### Base Eligibility
- No current residential property ownership.
- No ownership in recent lookback period (for example 5-10 years).
- Continuous rental history threshold (for example 5+ years).
- Income within target band.

### Priority Scoring
- Years renting
- Rent-to-income stress
- Dependents and household vulnerability
- Essential worker status
- Local employment/community connection

### Safeguards
- Exclude investment-property holders.
- Clawback or restrictions on rapid speculative resale.
- Public waitlist rules and independent review pathway.

### Bottom Takeaway
Income Title is targeted to exclusion, not privilege.

## Presenter Notes (1.5 minutes)
The eligibility design is deliberate: this pathway is for households who have been shut out of ownership despite sustained participation in the rental market.

We use objective base tests first, then transparent priority scoring to allocate scarce places fairly.

The anti-gaming rules are essential. They protect policy legitimacy and ensure public subsidy is not converted into short-term private windfall.

This keeps the model defensible both socially and fiscally.

## Data/Assumption Inputs to Verify Before Final Deck
- Confirm rental-history threshold and lookback period.
- Confirm target income bands by region.
- Confirm legal settings for resale restrictions/clawback treatment.
- Confirm priority weighting and appeals process design.

## Risks and Guardrails
- Risk: perceived unfairness in borderline cases.
- Guardrail: publish scoring rubric and independent appeal channel.

- Risk: administrative burden.
- Guardrail: digital verification pipeline linked to tax, tenancy, and title records.

- Risk: political framing as broad subsidy.
- Guardrail: keep eligibility narrow and exclusion-focused.
