# Slide 14 Plan: Worked Example - Middle and Higher Income Pathways

## Slide Objective
Show how higher earnings and wage growth accelerate time-to-completion under the same rule set.

## Time Allocation
- Target talk time: 1.5 minutes.
- Audience outcome: They should understand that the framework adapts with income and does not require separate products.

## Core Message
The same payment logic produces faster completion for higher-income households while preserving rule consistency.

## Supporting Proof Points
- Two pathway curves under shared mechanics.
- Faster cumulative progress for higher-income trajectory.
- Completion ranges can be compared directly.

## Recommended Slide Headline
Worked Example: Middle and Higher Income Pathways

## Recommended Subheadline
Same rules, different earning trajectories, different completion timing.

## Visual Concept
- Split-panel comparative chart with two curves.
- Right-side completion range chips.

## Layout (16:9)
- Top 20%: headline + subheadline.
- Middle 62%: comparative chart and chips.
- Bottom 18%: takeaway strip.

## On-Slide Copy (Draft)
- Middle-income pathway: steady progression under baseline assumptions.
- Higher-income pathway: accelerated completion via larger annual contribution.

## Presenter Notes (1.5 minutes)
This is a comparison slide, not a separate policy design.

Both pathways use the same rules. The only difference is income level and resulting contribution size.

That demonstrates scalability and fairness: the framework remains stable while completion timing naturally varies with capacity.
