# Slide 13 Plan: Worked Example - Moderate Income Household

## Slide Objective
Demonstrate a credible ownership pathway for a moderate-income household using simple, transparent assumptions.

## Time Allocation
- Target talk time: 2 minutes.
- Audience outcome: They should see that completion can be reached within a plausible timeframe.

## Core Message
Even moderate earners can progress to ownership on a predictable timeline under income-linked repayment rules.

## Supporting Proof Points
- Clear starting assumptions (income, payment rate, growth, floor).
- Year-by-year contributions show steady progress.
- Cumulative curve demonstrates completion trajectory.

## Recommended Slide Headline
Worked Example: Moderate Income Household

## Recommended Subheadline
Illustrative pathway from occupancy to ownership.

## Visual Concept
- Left: assumptions and mini table.
- Right: cumulative progress line with completion marker.

## Layout (16:9)
- Top 18%: headline + subheadline.
- Middle 66%: assumptions/table + chart.
- Bottom 16%: takeaway and source note.

## On-Slide Copy (Draft)
- Example assumptions: $95k starting income, 10% payment rate, 3% income growth.
- Cumulative contributions rise with income and move toward completion target.

## Presenter Notes (2 minutes)
This is an illustrative household pathway, not a guaranteed universal outcome.

The purpose is to show mechanics under realistic assumptions: moderate income, modest wage growth, and consistent participation.

The trajectory is understandable and auditable, which improves trust for both households and policymakers.
