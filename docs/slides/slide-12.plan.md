# Slide 12 Plan: Financial Mechanics for Households

## Slide Objective
Explain the repayment rules clearly so households understand how progress to ownership works without mortgage-style compounding stress.

## Time Allocation
- Target talk time: 2 minutes.
- Audience outcome: They should be able to restate the four core payment rules.

## Core Message
Repayments are income-linked, bounded by floor and cap rules, and convert to ownership when completion conditions are met.

## Supporting Proof Points
- Payment rate aligns to income (indicative 10-12%).
- Floor rule preserves minimum program progress.
- Cap/indexation rules define completion threshold transparently.
- Conversion trigger is rule-based and auditable.

## Recommended Subheadline

## On-Slide Copy (Draft)
- Rule 1: Payment rate = % of income (indicative 10-12%).
- Rule 2: Minimum annual floor protects progress.
- Rule 3: Indexed cap defines completion target.
- Rule 4: Conversion trigger transfers title to freehold.

## Presenter Notes (2 minutes)
This slide clarifies the household contract.

Unlike a conventional mortgage, payment burden is tied to income capacity. Households contribute as earnings allow, with a minimum floor and a transparent completion cap.

Completion is not discretionary. It is rule-based: once threshold conditions are met, title converts.

The objective is predictable progress with lower stress sensitivity to income volatility.
