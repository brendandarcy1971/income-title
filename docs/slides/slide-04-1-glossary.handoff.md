## Glossary Terms (Use Exactly)
### Cost-Formation Terms
- Raw land acquisition: purchase price of undeveloped land before rezoning and servicing.
- Rezoning/scarcity uplift: value uplift caused by planning status, constrained supply, and development permissions.
- Infrastructure and civil works: site servicing and subdivision works (roads, utilities, drainage, enabling works).
- Construction hard cost: direct dwelling build costs (materials, labor, core subcontract scope).
- Taxes, fees, compliance and approvals: statutory charges, application costs, and regulatory compliance costs.
- Overheads, risk and margin: developer and builder overhead allocation, risk pricing, and commercial margin.
- Final product price: end market price paid by the buyer for a completed home and lot outcome.

### Lending and Repayment Terms
- Lending multiple (for example 5x income): simplified borrowing-capacity ratio based on gross income.
- Maximum borrowing capacity: highest indicative loan amount permitted by lender settings and serviceability.
- Deposit: upfront buyer equity contributed to purchase.
- Loan required: purchase price minus deposit.
- Mortgage interest rate: annual loan rate used to calculate repayments.
- Loan term: repayment duration (for example 30 years).
- Monthly repayment: periodic mortgage payment implied by loan amount, rate, and term.
- Repayment burden (% income): annual mortgage repayments divided by gross household income.
