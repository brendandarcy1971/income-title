# Income Title Proposal: Slide-by-Slide Plan (45 Minutes)

## Audience
- Australian think tanks (e.g. Chifley, Menzies) and political decision-makers.

## Timing and Structure
- Total time: 45 minutes.
- Core presentation: 38-40 minutes.
- Discussion buffer: 5-7 minutes.
- Total slides: 22.

## Slide Plan

### 1. Title and Promise (1.5 min)
- Core message: Income Title can deliver detached homes with no deposit, no mortgage, and a realistic path to ownership.
- Evidence/data: Program headline numbers ($350k delivered cost target, 10-15 year ownership path for typical households).
- Visual: Hero image of new community with a single-line value proposition.
- Layout: Full-bleed visual + large headline + 3 numeric callouts.

### 2. Why This Matters Now (2 min)
- Core message: Australia has a housing affordability and access emergency.
- Evidence/data: Rent and purchase pressure trends; household stress; formation delays.
- Visual: Two-line chart (income vs housing cost growth).
- Layout: Left chart, right 3 bullets on social effects.

### 3. Human Consequences (1.5 min)
- Core message: Housing pressure is changing family, work, and mobility decisions.
- Evidence/data: Curated quotes from community and media coverage in `quotes.txt`.
- Visual: Quote wall with attribution tags.
- Layout: 2-3 quotes, minimal text, strong typography.

### 4. Diagnosis: What Is Actually Expensive? (2 min)
- Core message: Land system economics, not just construction, are the main affordability barrier.
- Additional point: Lending settings push buyers to maximise deposit and borrowing capacity, allowing prices, margins, and embedded costs to expand to fill available finance rather than track fundamental delivery economics.
- Evidence/data: Cost stack from `new_home_development.txt` (land + infra + taxes/charges + build).
- Visual: Stacked bar cost composition.
- Layout: Chart center, short interpretation below.

### 5. Why Current Settings Fail (2 min)
- Core message: Market-priced outputs and financing rules push ownership out of reach.
- Evidence/data: Deposit hurdle, mortgage horizon, repayment stress.
- Visual: Current pathway flow (Deposit -> Mortgage -> 30 years).
- Layout: Process diagram with pain points in red.

### 6. Policy Precedents We Can Borrow From (2 min)
- Core message: Income-linked and structured procurement systems already work in Australia.
- Evidence/data: HECS logic (income-linked repayment), PBS logic (single buyer/price discipline).
- Visual: Two-column precedent map.
- Layout: Left HECS principles, right PBS principles, center transfer-to-housing bridge.

### 7. The Income Title Model in One Slide (2 min)
- Core message: Occupy now, repay by income, convert to freehold on completion.
- Evidence/data: High-level mechanics from `income_title_outline.txt`.
- Visual: Lifecycle diagram (Entry -> Income-linked payments -> Title conversion).
- Layout: Horizontal 3-step schematic.

### 8. Product Definition: What Is Being Delivered (1.5 min)
- Core message: Standardised, family-suitable detached homes with consistent quality.
- Evidence/data: 3-bed, 2 living, 2 bath, single-storey, approx. 140-160 sqm.
- Visual: Annotated floorplan block.
- Layout: Left floorplan, right spec checklist.

### 9. Cost Structure and Target Economics (2 min)
- Core message: The $350k target is a designed outcome, not a hope.
- Evidence/data: Indicative split ($100k serviced land + $250k build).
- Visual: Donut or stacked bar with target thresholds.
- Layout: Chart + assumptions callout box.

### 10. Procurement Architecture (2 min)
- Core message: Government sets product, standards, and caps; private sector competes on delivery.
- Evidence/data: Open participation + compliance criteria from `procurement.txt`.
- Visual: System map (Government framework -> Multi-contractor delivery).
- Layout: Swimlane view across policy, procurement, construction.

### 11. Construction Pathways and Innovation (1.5 min)
- Core message: Prefab, onsite systemised, and 3D-assisted methods can all compete to one output standard.
- Evidence/data: Compatible multi-method framework from `procurement.txt`.
- Visual: Three-lane pathway graphic converging on one housing product.
- Layout: Tri-panel visual with common quality gate.

### 12. Financial Mechanics for Households (2 min)
- Core message: Repayments align to income and avoid compounding debt burden.
- Evidence/data: Suggested payment settings (10-12% of income, cap-based completion approach).
- Visual: Payment logic card set.
- Layout: Rule cards: payment rate, floor, cap/indexation, conversion trigger.

### 13. Worked Example: Moderate Income Household (2 min)
- Core message: Even moderate earners can reach ownership on a credible timeline.
- Evidence/data: Example trajectory from `income_title_outline.txt`.
- Visual: Year-by-year mini table + cumulative progress line.
- Layout: Left assumptions, right chart.

### 14. Worked Example: Middle and Higher Income Pathways (1.5 min)
- Core message: Wage growth accelerates ownership outcomes.
- Evidence/data: Two comparative examples with time-to-completion.
- Visual: Side-by-side curves.
- Layout: Split panel with completion ranges highlighted.

### 15. Mobility, Transfer, and Disposal Rules (1.5 min)
- Core message: Households can move without breaking the model.
- Evidence/data: Sell-before-completion settlement logic and balance treatment.
- Visual: Sale proceeds waterfall.
- Layout: Simple 4-step transaction diagram.

### 16. Title and Legal Architecture (2 min)
- Core message: Rights are clear through lifecycle from occupancy to full freehold.
- Evidence/data: Occupancy rights, transfer rights, conversion conditions, safeguards.
- Visual: Rights matrix by lifecycle stage.
- Layout: Table with stages as columns.

### 17. Government Roles and Approvals (2 min)
- Core message: Delivery requires a coordinated Commonwealth-state-local compact.
- Evidence/data: Who controls what: finance settings, planning, infrastructure, approvals.
- Visual: Responsibility matrix.
- Layout: 3-column RACI-style map.

### 18. Fiscal Position and Revolving Fund Logic (2 min)
- Core message: This is a policy investment with measurable recovery and recycling potential.
- Evidence/data: Per-dwelling economics and recovery profile from outline assumptions.
- Visual: Cashflow bridge (outlay -> repayments -> net policy gap).
- Layout: Waterfall chart + sensitivity note.

### 19. Risks and Mitigations (2 min)
- Core message: Key risks are known and manageable with clear design controls.
- Evidence/data: Market distortion, fairness, default risk, delivery quality, legal complexity.
- Visual: Risk heatmap.
- Layout: 5-row table (Risk | Impact | Control).

### 20. Common Objections and Direct Rebuttals (1.5 min)
- Core message: Most objections confuse this with existing subsidy models.
- Evidence/data: Distinction from grants, speculative first-home incentives, and uncontrolled supply-side subsidies.
- Visual: Myth vs fact panel.
- Layout: Two-column rebuttal cards.

### 21. Implementation Roadmap (2 min)
- Core message: Start with a bounded pilot, then scale based on measurable performance.
- Evidence/data: 100-day setup, 12-month pilot, 3-year scale plan.
- Visual: Timeline with decision gates.
- Layout: Horizontal roadmap with milestones.

### 22. Closing Ask (1 min)
- Core message: Commission design and pilot now to test the model in real market conditions.
- Evidence/data: Specific decisions requested from audience.
- Visual: 3-point action checklist.
- Layout: Clean final slide with concrete next steps.

## Production Checklist
- Ensure every slide has one message, one proof point, one visual.
- Keep text under 35 words on most slides.
- Use appendix slides for technical detail (legal definitions, modelling assumptions, procurement templates).
